# 26 — User Manual (Staff & Admin)

> **Draft outline.** This manual will be written against the real UI during ADMIN-* and REL-06 tasks. The content below defines what will be covered, so training needs are known early.

## Part A: Front-desk staff
1. **Opening checklist:** kiosk on, idle screen visible, camera light on, test "Tap to start", volume check.
2. **When a customer presses "Call staff":** the alert appears in admin and on WhatsApp. Go to the kiosk and enter your staff PIN to take over (continue the booking by touch, or cancel).
3. **Making a booking for a walk-in / phone caller:** Admin → Bookings → New. Same availability and pricing as the kiosk.
4. **Check-in:** search by booking reference (from the customer's WhatsApp/QR) or phone → mark Arrived / Completed / No-show.
5. **Cancel or reschedule:** Bookings → [booking] → Cancel / Reschedule (reason required).
6. **Enquiries:** Enquiries → New → call the customer → update the status (Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost).
7. **Kiosk problems:** see the troubleshooting table (no sound, camera not detecting, "offline" screen, stuck screen → nightly reboot or power cycle).
8. **Closing checklist.**

## Part B: Owner / manager
1. Packages: create, edit, activate/deactivate, features, guest limits.
2. Prices: weekday/weekend days, date overrides, holiday prices, promotions, tax setting. **Always use "Preview price" before saving.**
3. Schedule: business hours, holidays, special hours, blackout dates, manual blocks.
4. AI settings: assistant name, greeting, voice, languages, conversation limits, spend caps.
5. Reports: bookings, revenue, conversion funnel, popular packages, occasions, AI cost.
6. Users & roles: add staff, reset passwords, enable 2FA.
7. Privacy requests: customer data export / correction / erasure.

## Part C: Customer-facing notices (to be finalized with counsel)
Kiosk privacy notice (EN/HI), physical camera sign text, WhatsApp consent wording.
